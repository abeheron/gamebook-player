# Test and fight

Status: approved as P09, P13, and P22, and implemented on this branch. Not merged.

Test & fight, in the sidebar and the phone bar, opens the current section. A skill-test section shows the full test: formula, skill modifier, dice, and result. A fight section shows that fight. Any other section says it has no test or fight. Fights stay on their own full view. They do not use the compact panel.

The new sample fight is the pale moth on section 10: two rounds at a penalty of −1, from the passage, not from a prototype number. A round is a Talent test at that penalty. A miss is one Wound, which is Vitality −1. Vitality at 0 ends the fight at the passage's defeat section, and the remaining rounds are not rolled. Otherwise the fight is won by finishing the rounds, even if a round was missed. Fumbles, armour, shields, unarmed fighting, and Fortune are not in this change.

Pass and fail, on the compact panel and on this screen, appear only after the dice have settled. One destination is shown, the one that matches the roll.

The full test can take typed dice. Each face must be a whole number from 1 to 6. Those faces are the roll. They are not replaced by a random roll. The compact panel does not offer typed dice. Closing it and opening the full test still share one unsettled or settled roll.

The save shape is unchanged. No migration was required.

## Rules labels now in this build

These two summary lines no longer say "Not yet in this app":

- A Wound reduces Vitality by one. Vitality at zero ends the adventure, unless that passage says otherwise. (p. 8)
- Each fight round is a Talent test at the stated penalty. A failed round is one Wound. Finish the rounds and still be standing to win. (p. 10)

The fumble sentence was split onto its own line and still says not yet. Shields, unarmed fighting, and armour stay marked not yet.
